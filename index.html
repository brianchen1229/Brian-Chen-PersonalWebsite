About me

Welcome! I am a Research Assistant at the Institute of Statistical Science, Academia Sinica, advised by Prof. Hsin-Cheng Huang .

My research interests broadly encompass machine learning, deep learning, and Bayesian statistics, with a recent focus on denoising diffusion probabilistic models and catalytic priors.

I received my B.B.A. in Financial Engineering and Actuarial Mathematics from Soochow University in 2025. I graduated one year early with a cumulative GPA of 4.0/4.0 and ranked first in my class.
